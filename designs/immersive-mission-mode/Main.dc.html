Immersive Mission Mode
The learner participates in the already-authored story. Same rounds, same arc, same word pool — but at 2–3 acts the scene stops, Halm frames the situation, and the learner picks one of three candidate lines. The tapped line explains itself instantly. Two taps per act, never a chat.

One glance, one goal
Scene, situation and all three lines are on screen together. The mission line stays pinned while choosing.
Instant reveal
Rationales arrive with the options in one call. Tap → explanation, synchronously. No spinner, no network, no grading phase.
Two movements per act
One tap to answer, one to continue — right or wrong. Wrong pick reveals both rationales in the same frame; no retry loop.
A chat skin, a bounded beat
The story reads as a feed and the hint rises over a real composer — but the beat stays bounded: three vetted lines as the floor, phrases and help one tap away, typed lines as the gated PCC-graded tier.
