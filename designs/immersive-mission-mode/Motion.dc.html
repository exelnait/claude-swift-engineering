REDUCE MOTION · VOICEOVER
Everything above collapses through the shipped seams — motionAnimation / withMotion, never a bare .animation. The act assembles at once (revealAll), no stagger, no scale; the sibling opacity recede stays (it is information, not decoration). The morph flight becomes a crossfade. VoiceOver: three real buttons ≥44 pt, verdict carried by accessibilityValue + glyph, never colour alone; focus lands on the reveal when it lands.
WHAT NEVER ANIMATES
No spinner at tap time — ever. No AI gradient on options or reveals (reserved for intelligence affordances). No confetti mid-story; the flame palette waits for the recap. No painted keyboard, ever — the system keyboard’s space is left honest. Hint copy swaps by opacity, never by height.
