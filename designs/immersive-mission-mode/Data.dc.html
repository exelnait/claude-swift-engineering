Where the words come from
Three tiers — every act always has a floor, and tapping a line never waits on a model.
1 · Ships in the bundle
halm_data_pipeline · built & checked offline
•The whole story — round texts, three-round arc, word pool, present lemmas. The pick never branches it.
•TTS audio + word timings, pre-rendered per round — another reason the plot can’t branch.
•Can-do line per story (DialogueShape) — becomes the mission line and the derived act framing.
•acts[] (optional, W6) — hand-authored situations for curated stories; override the derived floor, never required.
The floor: situation framing derives from can-do + word pool + round text — zero new data. A story with no authored acts still plays every act.
2 · Generated ahead of play
PCC · one structured call per act · deep reasoning
•Act 1 rides the loading phase — plan + rounds + TTS already run there; one extra call is absorbable.
•Acts 2–3 prefetch during round 1 in a background task. After they land the lesson is network-independent.
•Each call returns everything: situation line (native), 3 lines (German), the marked correct one, 3 rationales (native, ≤160 chars).
•Facts come from tools, not the model: lexicon (article/plural/POS/CEFR) + case + conjugation resolvers — prose is formulated around verified facts.
Answer time is model-free: the tap compares a stored boolean. Instant reveal, offline, no false-“correct” failure mode.
3 · When the cloud can’t help
decided once at lesson start · never mid-beat
•PCC unusable at start (offline · quota · cloud turned off) → the mission entry is absent from the preview, with one quiet line why. The story still plays as Listen.
•One act’s prefetch missed → that round plays as plain Listen; the mission goes on. No spinner mid-story, ever.
•On-device tier (later, W5): FM writes shorter lines with the lexicon tool only; the rationale becomes a reason category the pack renders as a sentence — classification, not prose. Needs the §9 owner call first.
•Typed lines (the composer) are the gated §8 tier: graded on the go by PCC with redaction and a hard char cap — the one act-time model call. The three vetted lines stay the offline floor.
Never: a present-and-failing mode, a retry that regenerates, or FM judging free text. Degrade by absence, not by breakage.

ONE CALL PER ACT — THE WHOLE PAYLOAD
StoryActOptions situation "Your turn — you'd like two bread rolls." options[3] // exactly three text "Ich hätte gern zwei Brötchen." // German isCorrect true // exactly one true rationale "‘hätte gern’ keeps it polite…" // native, ≤160 chars
Correctness is decided at generation time, checked at tap time by boolean compare. Tools attached to the call: lexicon lookup, case fact, conjugation fact — four max.
WHAT KEEPS THE OPTIONS HONEST
•Distractors wrong for a teachable reason — register, case, word order — never nonsense, never solvable by length or by being the only formal one (eval-locked).
•Rationales say “why den, not der” in plain words — never “Grammatik: Akkusativ”.
•What leaves the device: scene text, framing, 3–5 constructions, CEFR, register, two language codes. Never a name, profile or history.
•Nothing persists — options live for the session and are dropped on exit. Wrong picks never touch FSRS; Keep in the word popup stays the only way a word enters review.
